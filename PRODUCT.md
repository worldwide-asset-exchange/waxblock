# Product

## Register

product

## Users

WAX blockchain users, wallet users, validators, and developers who need to inspect accounts, blocks, transactions, resources, votes, NFTs, and wallet actions while working with WAX mainnet, testnet, or localnet.

## Product Purpose

WaxBlock is a WAX block explorer with an integrated web wallet. It helps users monitor chain activity, validate transactions, inspect account state, and complete wallet tasks such as transfers, voting, name bids, permissions, and utilities.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a dense explorer and wallet tool, with a clear WAX identity and predictable controls.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style landing page composition inside task surfaces, decorative controls that reduce scan speed, and layouts that hide critical explorer or wallet actions at common viewport widths.

## Design Principles

- Keep navigation and account context reachable at all supported sizes.
- Favor dense, scannable information over decorative space.
- Preserve WAX identity while keeping product controls familiar.
- Make wallet and explorer actions predictable, explicit, and resilient.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast and keyboard access. Preserve visible focus states, support reduced motion where animation exists, and avoid interaction patterns that depend on pointer hover alone on compact screens.
